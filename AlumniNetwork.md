The NEH Alumni Network is a list serve that allows former staff and interns at NEH to stay connected and share news.

Learn more: [https://groups.google.com/u/6/g/nehalumni](https://groups.google.com/u/6/g/nehalumni)

The network was established in May 2025 by former NEH staff member Hannah Alpert-Abrams, in collaboration with current staff members at NEH. You can join by visiting the site linked above or emailing us at friendsofneh@gmail.com.
